# Phase 4 — Monthly STAFF Payrolls

## Objective

Add an independent monthly STAFF remuneration-control module based on the existing Worker registry, without changing weekly GeneralPayroll behavior.

## Authorized scope

- Remove `WorkerType.unspecified` safely and require an explicit worker type.
- Add monthly STAFF payroll Prisma models and a versioned, unapplied migration.
- Add the NestJS staff-payroll module, permissions, calculations, Excel export, and focused tests.
- Add the React STAFF people, monthly periods, and detail screens; routes, navigation, permissions, and focused contract tests.
- Preserve all pre-existing uncommitted work and do not commit, push, change branches, use worktrees, or apply migrations.

## Constraints

- STAFF eligibility is derived only from active, non-deleted Workers of type engineer, administrator, or manager.
- Financial access requires both `staffPayroll.*` and `finance.view` as applicable.
- Historical entries are immutable snapshots with respect to later Worker/profile changes.
- Monetary calculations use exact decimal arithmetic and never silently default an unconfigured salary to zero.
- GeneralPayroll and its weekly schema/behavior remain unchanged.

- Route: direct execution under the exact-model-unavailable path; the configured `gpt-6.1-luna` explorer/writer model is unavailable, so no substitute model will be used.

## Acceptance criteria

- Eligible Workers appear automatically in STAFF and ineligible Workers do not.
- Missing remuneration is visible and blocks monthly insertion rather than becoming PEN 0.
- Monthly periods are unique; entries are unique per Worker and preserve snapshots.
- Gross/net totals are exact, nonnegative, and enforced by the backend.
- Excel export and all economic endpoints/actions enforce the approved permissions.
- Existing weekly payroll routes and implementation remain operational and unmodified.
